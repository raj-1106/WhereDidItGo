# 💸 WhereDidItGo

> Because your money disappears… and you deserve answers.

**WhereDidItGo** is a sleek and minimal expense tracking web app that helps you understand exactly where your money goes — without overwhelming you.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🧾 **Track Every Expense**  
  Quickly add and manage your daily spending.

- 💰 **Monthly Salary & Budget Overview**  
  Know how much you earned, spent, and saved.

- 📊 **Visual Insights**
  - Category-wise breakdown (Pie Chart)
  - Daily spending trends (Bar Chart)

- 🏷️ **Smart Categorization**  
  Organize expenses into meaningful categories.

- ⚡ **Fast & Clean UI**  
  No clutter. Just clarity.

- 🔄 **Real-time Updates**  
  Add, delete, and see changes instantly.

- 📄 **Pagination Ready**  
  Smoothly browse through large expense histories.

---

## 🧠 The Idea

Ever checked your balance and thought:

> *“Wait… where did it go?”*

This app answers that question.

---
